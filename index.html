Risk detection

Risks you might not have considered

The agent proactively surfaces second-order effects that are easy to miss when you're close to an idea.

📉

Conversion rate drop

Changes to pricing, UX, or funnel structure that could reduce the % of visitors becoming customers.

🔀

Cannibalization

New offerings that pull revenue away from existing products or customer segments instead of growing the pie.

💸

Hidden cost exposure

Infrastructure, support, compliance, or operational costs that scale faster than revenue.

⏳

Payback period

How long until the initiative breaks even — and the cash flow implications in the interim.

🏷️

Brand & pricing dilution

Signals sent to the market that could erode perceived value or trigger competitive responses.

🔄

Churn acceleration

Changes that may increase existing customer churn — even if the initiative looks positive in isolation.
